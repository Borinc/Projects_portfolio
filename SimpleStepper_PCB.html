Simple stepper driver PCB

2026

PCB

This project was a small project to do during train rides. The focus was to make a building block that would reduce the time integrating a stepper motor in future projects drastically. The requirement was to make it as simple as possible and to finish it as quick as possible. Looking back I am happy with the effectiveness of the PCB and how quick I finished the project. Everything is designed in KiCAD.

Assembled

Bare PCB assembled on back of stepper motor.
